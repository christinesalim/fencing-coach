Parry 4 Riposte - correct form
April 8, 2026  ·  2:34  ·  Defense   Transcribed
parry 4 ✕ riposte ✕ Coach Ziad ✕
Description
Good example of keeping blade contact through the parry and finishing with a direct riposte to the chest. Coach Ziad emphasized keeping the elbow high and not dropping the point during the parry.
Transcript
OK so watch this one. See how he keeps the blade... there you go. That's what I want to see. The blade stays in contact the whole way through the parry. Don't let it bounce off. And then the riposte goes straight - don't circle, don't hesitate, just straight to the chest. Again... yes! Perfect. See the difference? Before you were dropping your point during the parry and then trying to come back up for the riposte. That's too slow. Keep the point up, keep contact, and then just extend. The riposte should feel like one motion with the parry, not two separate actions. One more time...
